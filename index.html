HOW TO PLAY

Move: Arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or your selected mobile control (D-pad or joystick).

Eat: Collect green food for 10 points and grow.

Power-ups: Collect purple items for temporary abilities: speed, slow motion, shield, ghost, double points, bonus points, or magnet.

Survive: Avoid your body, walls, and obstacles. Shield absorbs one collision.
